About & disclaimer

          

            What ExamGo AI installs, what it can't promise, and who it doesn't
            speak for.
          

          

            

              

                What this app installs and runs, in plain words: ExamGo AI
                itself, plus Ollama — a free, open-source program that runs
                AI models directly on your computer — and any AI models you

                owners). Models are fetched from the public Ollama library and
                stored, along with your question sets, scores, and settings, in
                this app's own data folder on your PC. Except for one-time
                downloads of Ollama and AI models during setup, this app sends
                nothing to any server and needs no account.
              

              

                Accuracy: all questions and explanations are generated by
                AI and can be incomplete, outdated, or simply wrong. They are

                Uninstalling: removing ExamGo AI does not automatically

                several gigabytes each. Remove them yourself via Windows'
                "Add or remove programs" (Ollama) and by deleting this app's
